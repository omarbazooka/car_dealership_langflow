# Dealership FAQ — Demo Data

This document is synthetic demo knowledge for the technical assessment prototype.

## Can I reserve a vehicle?
The prototype currently supports test-drive requests and sales leads. A reservation or purchase order is not implemented unless an additional business tool is added.

## Are listed cars guaranteed to be available?
No. Search results represent records in the prototype inventory dataset. The assistant should not claim real-time physical availability unless a dedicated availability field or service is connected.

## Can I speak with sales?
Yes. The assistant can create a sales lead after collecting the customer's name and phone number. A car ID can be included when the customer has selected a vehicle.
